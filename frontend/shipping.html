Important Delivery Notes

  • Orders placed before 2:00 PM on weekdays are processed the same day.
  • Orders placed after 2pm on Fridays, weekends, or public holidays are processed the next working day.
  • Someone must be present to receive delivery. If unavailable, our courier will reattempt twice more at no extra charge.
  • After 3 failed delivery attempts, your order returns to our warehouse and you'll be contacted to reschedule.
  • Unclaimed orders held more than 7 days are automatically refunded minus the original shipping fee.
  • We do not deliver to PO boxes — a physical address with landmark is required.
  • For commercial / bulk orders above ₦500,000, contact us for tailored logistics arrangements.

📞 Missed your delivery? WhatsApp us within 48 hours to reschedule at no extra charge — +234 800 VOLAHUB.
