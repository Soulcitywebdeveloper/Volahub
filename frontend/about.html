Our Story

From Idea to Nigeria's FMCG Hub

How VolaHub grew from a small Lagos startup to a nationwide FMCG platform in just two years.

💡
January 2022
The Idea is Born
Co-founders Adebayo and Chioma were frustrated by the difficulty of getting quality household products delivered reliably in Lagos. They decided to build the solution themselves.
🚀
June 2022
VolaHub Launches in Lagos
We launched with just 80 products and 3 delivery drivers. Our first 100 customers came entirely from word-of-mouth. We fulfilled every single order on time.
📈
March 2023
1,000 Customers & Series A
We hit our first 1,000 active customers milestone and raised our Series A funding round, allowing us to expand our warehouse and product range significantly.
🇳🇬
September 2023
Nationwide Expansion
VolaHub expanded to Abuja, Port Harcourt, and Kano. We partnered with regional logistics companies to enable nationwide delivery to all 36 states.
🌟
2024 – Present
12,000+ Customers & Growing
Today VolaHub serves over 12,000 customers with 500+ products, a 98.4% satisfaction rate, and a growing team of 120+ passionate Nigerians dedicated to our mission.
