Our Story

From Lagos Startup
to Nigeria's FMCG Hub

How VolaHub grew from a bold idea into a nationwide platform in just two years.

💡
January 2022
The Idea is Born
Co-founders Adebayo and Chioma were frustrated by how hard it was to get quality household products delivered reliably in Lagos. They decided to build the solution themselves.
🚀
June 2022
VolaHub Launches in Lagos
We launched with just 80 products and 3 delivery drivers. Our first 100 customers came entirely from word-of-mouth — and we fulfilled every single order on time.
📈
March 2023
1,000 Customers & Series A Funding
We hit our first 1,000 active customers milestone and closed our Series A funding round, enabling us to expand our warehouse capacity and triple our product range.
🇳🇬
September 2023
Nationwide Expansion
VolaHub expanded to Abuja, Port Harcourt, and Kano. We partnered with regional logistics companies to enable 48-hour delivery to all 36 Nigerian states and the FCT.
🌟
2024 – Present
12,000+ Customers & Still Growing
Today VolaHub serves over 12,000 customers with 500+ products, a 98.4% customer satisfaction rate, and a passionate team of 120+ Nigerians dedicated to our mission every day.
