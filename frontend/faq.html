🧾 Orders
How do I place an order on VolaHub?▼
Browse our product catalogue, add items to your cart, then click "Proceed to Checkout." You'll be asked to sign in or create a free account, enter your delivery address, choose a payment method, and confirm. You receive an email confirmation immediately and can track your order in My Orders.
Can I modify or cancel my order after placing it?▼
Yes — within 1 hour of placing your order. Contact us via WhatsApp (+234 800 VOLAHUB) immediately. After that, your order enters processing and can no longer be changed. If it has already been dispatched, you will need to use our 7-day returns process once delivered.
How do I track my order?▼
Two ways: (1) Log into your account and visit My Orders for real-time status. (2) Use the "Track an Order" tab with just your order number and email — no login needed. You also receive SMS and email notifications at each status change.
What do the order statuses mean?▼
  • Pending — Received, awaiting confirmation
  • Confirmed — Verified and accepted by our team
  • Processing — Being picked and packed in our warehouse
  • Shipped — Handed over to our logistics partner, in transit
  • Delivered — Successfully delivered to your address
  • Cancelled — Order was cancelled before dispatch
I didn't receive a confirmation email. What should I do?▼
First check your spam or junk mail folder — confirmation emails sometimes land there. Then log in and check My Orders. If your order appears there, it was placed successfully and we can resend the email. If it doesn't appear, your payment may not have completed — contact us at support@volahub.com with your transaction reference.
Can I place a bulk or corporate order?▼
Yes! VolaHub has a dedicated B2B programme for businesses ordering large quantities. You get custom pricing (minimum 15% discount), invoice-based billing, and a dedicated account manager. Email b2b@volahub.com or visit our Partnerships page.
🚚 Delivery
How long does delivery take?▼
Standard delivery is 2–5 business days depending on your location. Lagos, Abuja, Port Harcourt, and Kano receive delivery in 1–2 days. Express delivery (available in hub cities) delivers same-day or next-day. See our full shipping guide for a state-by-state breakdown.
Do you deliver to my state?▼
Yes — VolaHub delivers to all 36 states and the FCT. Some rural local government areas may require an extra day. If you're unsure about your exact area, contact us before ordering and we'll confirm availability and timing.
How much does shipping cost?▼
Standard delivery is ₦1,500 for orders under ₦50,000. Orders above ₦50,000 ship FREE automatically — no coupon needed. Express delivery in hub cities is ₦3,500. The correct fee is always shown at checkout before you confirm.
What happens if I miss my delivery?▼
Our courier will attempt delivery 3 times total. After each missed attempt, you'll receive an SMS and email notification. After 3 failed attempts, the order returns to our warehouse. Contact us within 48 hours on WhatsApp to reschedule at no extra charge. After 7 days, unclaimed orders are automatically refunded.
Can I change my delivery address after ordering?▼
Only before the order is dispatched. Contact us immediately via WhatsApp (+234 800 VOLAHUB). Once our courier has picked up the package, the address cannot be changed and you would need to reschedule redelivery after the first attempt.
💳 Payment
What payment methods do you accept?▼
  • Card Payment — Visa, Mastercard, Verve (online)
  • Bank Transfer — Direct transfer, we confirm within 30 minutes
  • Pay on Delivery — Cash or POS machine at your door
  • Mobile Money — Opay, Palmpay, Kuda, and other Nigerian fintechs
Is it safe to pay on VolaHub?▼
Completely safe. All card transactions go through 256-bit SSL encryption via certified Nigerian payment gateways (Paystack/Flutterwave). We never store your card details on our servers. If you'd prefer not to pay online, Pay on Delivery (cash or POS) is always available.
I was charged but my order didn't go through. What happened?▼
This is usually a temporary authorisation hold — the charge reverses automatically in 3–5 business days. Check My Orders: if no order appears, the transaction failed and any hold will reverse automatically. If it doesn't reverse within 7 days, email us at support@volahub.com with your bank transaction reference and we'll investigate immediately.
Do you offer buy now, pay later or installments?▼
Not currently, but we are actively evaluating BNPL integration with Nigerian fintech partners. Join our mailing list to be notified when it launches — or contact us for corporate credit terms on bulk orders above ₦200,000.
↩️ Returns & Refunds
What is your returns policy?▼
You can return most eligible items within 7 days of delivery. Items must be unopened, in original condition and packaging. Read the full Returns Policy for the complete list of eligible and non-returnable items.
How long do refunds take?▼
After we receive and inspect your return (1–2 business days), we approve and process the refund. Card refunds take 3–5 business days to appear. Bank transfer refunds take 1–3 business days. You'll receive email confirmation when the refund is initiated.
I received a damaged or wrong item — what do I do?▼
Take photos of the item and packaging and contact us immediately via WhatsApp or email. For damaged or wrong items, we cover all return shipping costs and process a full refund or free replacement within 24 hours of your report. You don't need to wait for the 7-day window — report it as soon as you notice.
Can I exchange instead of getting a refund?▼
Yes! We're happy to exchange any eligible item for a different variant, size, or alternative product. Contact us via WhatsApp with your order number and the item you'd like instead, and we'll check stock availability within 24 hours.
👤 Account
Do I need an account to shop?▼
You need a free account to place orders and track deliveries. Creating one takes under 60 seconds — just your name, email, and a password. Your account also gives you access to order history, saved addresses, and exclusive member-only discounts.
I forgot my password. How do I reset it?▼
Click "Sign In" on the store, then tap "Forgot Password." Enter the email address on your account and we'll send a reset link. If you don't receive it within 5 minutes, check your spam folder. Still nothing? Contact support@volahub.com and we'll sort it out manually.
How do I delete my VolaHub account?▼
Email support@volahub.com with the subject "Account Deletion Request" from your registered email address. We'll process your request within 7 business days. Note: account deletion is permanent and removes all your order history, saved addresses, and profile data.
Is my personal data safe with VolaHub?▼
Yes. We comply with Nigeria's data protection regulations (NDPR). Your personal information is encrypted, never sold to third parties, and used only to fulfil your orders and improve your shopping experience. Read our Privacy Policy for full details.
📦 Products
Are all products on VolaHub authentic?▼
Absolutely. Every product is sourced directly from verified manufacturers and authorised Nigerian distributors. We perform quality checks on every batch received and immediately remove any products that don't pass. If you ever receive a product you suspect is counterfeit, contact us and you'll receive a full refund immediately — no questions asked.
How do I know if a product is in stock?▼
Every product page shows live stock status: "In Stock," "Only X left" (low stock warning), or "Out of Stock." Stock updates in real time as orders are placed. Out-of-stock items are restocked regularly — check back or contact us to request a restock notification.
Do you sell expired products?▼
Never. Every perishable product we dispatch has a minimum 3-month remaining shelf life. Our warehouse team checks expiry dates on every outbound order. If you ever receive an expired product, contact us immediately for a full refund and free replacement — this is one of our highest-priority issues.
Can I request a product that isn't listed?▼
Yes! Email products@volahub.com with the product name, brand, and how many units you need. Our procurement team reviews all requests weekly and frequently adds popular suggestions to our catalogue. For large quantities (case quantities or more), we can often source and deliver within 5–7 business days.
