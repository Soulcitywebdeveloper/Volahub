Our Return Promise

At VolaHub, your satisfaction is our highest priority. We stand behind every product we sell. If you are not 100% satisfied with your purchase for any reason, we make it easy to return items and receive a full refund — no stress, no lengthy procedures.

🎯 You have 7 calendar days from the date of confirmed delivery to initiate a return on any eligible item.

We handle thousands of deliveries across Nigeria every week. Our quality control is rigorous, but we know issues can happen. When they do, our team resolves them fast — most refunds are approved and processed within 48 hours of us receiving the returned item.

We also pro-actively handle issues you might not even report: if our system flags an order as potentially damaged in transit, we'll reach out to you before you even contact us.

What Can Be Returned?

Items are eligible for return if all of the following apply:

  • The return is initiated within 7 calendar days of confirmed delivery
  • The item is in original condition — unopened and unused, with original packaging intact
  • You can provide your order number or delivery confirmation
  • The item is not on the non-returnable list below

We also always accept returns (even for normally non-returnable categories) when:

  • ✅ Damaged on arrival — broken seal, crushed packaging, or physical damage
  • ✅ Wrong item delivered — you received a different product than what you ordered
  • ✅ Expired on arrival — the product was past its best-before date when delivered
  • ✅ Counterfeit / not genuine — product does not match the brand advertised

📸 Pro tip: Take a short video or photos of the item and its packaging the moment it arrives. This speeds up your return claim by 80%.

How to Start a Return

The process is quick — most customers complete it in under 3 minutes:

1

WhatsApp or email us with your order number and a brief reason for the return. Within 7 days of delivery.
2
Get Approved

3

Bring to any of our partner drop-off points, or we arrange free collection for valid damage/wrong item claims.

Refund Timeline

Once we receive your returned item, the refund process follows this timeline:

Day 0 — Item Received
Our returns team receives and logs your return at the warehouse.
Day 1–2 — Inspection
Quality team inspects the item and confirms condition matches your claim.
Day 2 — Approval Notification
You receive an email or WhatsApp confirmation that your refund has been approved.
Day 3–7 — Refund Processed
Money returned to your original payment method. Cards take 3–5 days; bank transfers take 1–3 days.

💳 Refunds always go back to the original payment method. For Pay on Delivery returns, we do a direct bank transfer within 2 business days of approval — provide your account details when contacting us.

⏱ If your refund has not appeared after 7 business days, contact your bank first — then reach out to us at returns@volahub.com with your RAC number.

Exchanges

We are happy to exchange any eligible item for a different variant, size, or alternative product of equal or greater value. To request an exchange:

  • Contact us through any of the return channels above
  • Specify the item you'd like in exchange
  • We'll confirm stock availability within 24 hours
  • For products of higher value, you pay the difference; for lower value, we refund the difference

If your preferred exchange product is out of stock, we will issue a full refund instead — you never lose out.

Non-Returnable Items

The following items are excluded from change-of-mind returns due to hygiene, safety, or perishability regulations:

  • 🚫 Opened food and beverage products
  • 🚫 Opened personal care products (deodorant, razors, sanitary products, skincare once used)
  • 🚫 Opened baby formula or infant feeding products
  • 🚫 Products with broken seals that were intact on delivery
  • 🚫 Products without original or substantially intact packaging
  • 🚫 Items marked "Final Sale" during a flash or clearance promotion

⚠️ Exception: If you received a damaged, expired, or counterfeit version of any of the above items, we still process a full return or replacement — these exclusions only apply to change-of-mind returns on properly delivered goods.
